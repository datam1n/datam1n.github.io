## DataMin @ MTNE 2025

The DataMin research group is pleased to announce its participation to the 16th Hungarian
Science Day in Transylvania (MTNE 2025), held between November 28-30, 2025 at the Sapientia University in Târgu Mureș, Romania.

Multiple team members presented their recent research results in various fields. 
Furthermore, some of our members were also involved in the organization of the event:

* [Bodó Zalán](/members/zalan-bodo) - Program Committee member
* [Szenkovits Annamária](/members/annamaria-szenkovits) - Session chair, Informatics section
